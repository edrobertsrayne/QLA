# QLA

QLA (Question-Level Analysis) helps a classroom teacher turn an exam paper and its markscheme into structured, spec-mapped data, then into cohort-level insight and individual student feedback.

## Language

**Markscheme**:
The exam board's official rubric for an assessment — the marks-allocation and answer-criteria document published alongside a paper. An *input* to Phase 1.
_Avoid_: Mark sheet, answer key (when referring to this specific document)

**Marksheet**:
A QLA-generated record of one cohort's marks, per student per question, built in Phase 2 to support strengths/weaknesses analysis. An *output* the app builds, not an uploaded document.
_Avoid_: Markscheme, gradebook

**Assessment paper**:
The exam paper itself (the questions), uploaded alongside its markscheme in Phase 1.

**Exam specification**:
The exam board's syllabus document defining topic/skill codes (e.g. AQA 8035) that questions are mapped against. Maintained per board/subject/tier/version, fetched once and cached rather than re-uploaded per assessment.
_Avoid_: Spec (informal shorthand is fine in conversation, not as a defined term)

**Spec code**:
A single topic/skill/assessment-objective identifier from an exam specification, used to tag a question or sub-point in the Phase 1 breakdown.
